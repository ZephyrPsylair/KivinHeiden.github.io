The brief

Rogue Rush is an endless runner developed in Unreal Engine where reflexes meet fast decision-making. Players navigate a constantly moving path using three-lane movement while reacting to dynamically placed obstacles and the changing environment.

Core gameplay

Three-lane movement

Players switch between three lanes to navigate the level and position themselves around incoming obstacles.

Endless running

The character continuously moves forward, keeping the focus on quick reactions and maintaining momentum.

Dynamic obstacles

Obstacles create constantly changing situations that require players to quickly identify a safe lane and react accordingly.

Rogue Rush demonstrating the 3-lane movement and obstacle avoidance system.

Systems & Technical Highlights

Dynamic obstacle system

Implemented a system for managing and placing obstacles throughout the runner's gameplay path.

Three-lane movement

Developed the lane-based movement system to provide responsive switching between the three available lanes.

Custom curved shader

Created a custom shader to produce the curved visual effect used throughout the game's environment.
